(01) Our journey

Established in 2019, Rasheed Holdings Intl was built on a foundation in cementitious product trading and shipping.

Our first subsidiary, Cem Asia Trading, founded in 2012, is a specialized cement trading company that has grown to serve an extensive global network and remains dedicated to meeting the demands of the cement ecosystem.

As we grew, we embraced change and innovation to expand our portfolio to include Rasheed International Estates in 2024. This subsidiary owns and manages residential properties across Malta.

Our journey is marked by a commitment to create shareholder value while contributing positively to the communities we serve.
